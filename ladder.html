The Ladder

The desk's top keys, ranked by their record. The order is published and mechanical: grade first — Pillar, then Proven, then Probation — then most graduations, then highest best ATH, then most launches. There is no score and no hand on the scale: the order is the record, read from the same snapshot the launch page and the public files use. A grade is a record under a published method (the rules · the methodology), not a verdict on a person and not a promise about any coin.

Best ATH is the highest market cap in the desk's price record.
