How grading works

Every alert is written to the catch log before it is delivered. The builder then grades his own alerts in a nightly verdict digest: mattered, noise, or false. Verdicts land on the events in the log, where anyone can check them against the chain.

The after-the-fact chain review hunts for the moves the watcher should have caught and did not. What it finds goes in the same log, in the same format, as a miss. Corrections are appended, never edited in. The record you see is the record there is.
