Security, in plain terms.

TELLS takes no custody of anything. It holds no keys, moves no funds, and asks for very little. This page says exactly what that means: what TELLS never asks for, what joining stores, how the alerts work, and the only channels that are really us.

What TELLS never asks for

TELLS will never ask for your seed phrase. It will never ask for your private keys, your password, or your money. It will never ask you to connect a wallet.

There is nothing to connect and nothing to deposit. TELLS reads the public chain. It does not need anything from your wallet, and nobody from TELLS will ever ask for these things in a message, a form, or a call.

If anyone asks in TELLS's name, it is not TELLS. Report it and leave.

What joining stores

When you tap into the alerts bot, TELLS records two things: your Telegram ID, so the bot can send to you, and which page you came from, so we know which door worked. That is the whole file.

No email address. No wallet address. No payment details. Joining collects none of them.

Send /stop to the bot and the alerts stop.

How alerts work

TELLS reads public chain data only: wallets, balances, and transactions anyone can see on the chain. It takes no custody of anything, and it cannot move funds. Not yours, not anyone's.

Every alert is written to the public catch log before it is delivered, so the record exists whether the alert was right or wrong. When TELLS is wrong, the correction is published in the same place. Misses are logged there too.

One line of separation

TELLS is not BONDED. A TELLS alert says something moved. It says nothing about any bond. The two products share a builder and nothing else.
